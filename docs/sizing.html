CRITICAL CALIBRATION

10mm Lever Belt Sizing Protocol

IMPORTANT: Do not use your trouser waist size. Powerlifting belts are worn over the abdominal wall directly across the navel. Pull a flexible tape measure tight around your navel while braced to determine your exact circumference.

  • Small (S): 26" to 31" navel circumference
  • Medium (M): 30" to 35" navel circumference
  • Large (L): 34" to 39" navel circumference
  • X-Large (XL): 38" to 43" navel circumference
  • 2X-Large (XXL): 42" to 47" navel circumference
